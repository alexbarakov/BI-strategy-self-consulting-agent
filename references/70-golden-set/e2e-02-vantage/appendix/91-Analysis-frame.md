# The seven blocks — what each established and where it went

The frame is the instrument, not the document. Two lines per block: what the analysis found, and which stream or initiative carries it.

| # | Block | What it established | Where it went |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Business foundation | Subscription economics: the board is graded on twelve commercial metrics, and those twelve are what everything else has to be true about | narrowed S1 from "a semantic layer" to "twelve definitions" |
| **2** | Landscape assessment | Tools strong, meaning absent. Centralized reporting does not exist and will not | [[01-Context]], [[appendix/90-Diagnostics|Diagnostics]] |
| **3** | Target operating model | The single version of truth has to come from definitions with owners, not from a central team | S1, and the deliberate non-coverage of P7 in [[02-Streams]] |
| **4** | Process foundation | No verify gate, no eval, no refusal path. The few-shots are the de facto semantic layer | S3, S4 |
| **5** | Technology | Nothing needs buying. The gaps are a semantic layer and an identity for the assistant — both build, not buy | I3, I6 |
| **6** | Operations | Governance at 0.5 FTE cannot be made real this year under a hiring freeze | the mandate narrowed to twelve metrics; P7 recorded as uncovered |
| **7** | Change management | 400 users are about to be given fewer answers, and that has to be sold before it is shipped | R2, and the cost-of-narrowing number in [[03-Initiatives]] |

## Blocks that reached no stream

**None went entirely unused**, but block 6 reached only a narrowed form. That is a deliberate decision under a hiring freeze rather than unfinished analysis, and it appears on the front page under what we do not do.
