# 91. Analysis frame

The seven blocks of the frame are a working instrument. The document carries the result; this page records what each block established and where it went.

| Block | What we established | Where it went |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Business foundation** | Domains are not defined, there are no owners. Key entities: sales, stock, loyalty. Consumers: commerce, categories, logistics, finance | Problems P1, P3 → S1, S2 |
| **2. Landscape assessment** | Average level 1.5; AI readiness zero on three dimensions out of five; adoption not measured | [[90-Diagnostics]], problem P4 |
| **3. Target operating model** | Today it is pure centralized delivery with pockets of spontaneous self-service. The target is guided for executives, governed self-service for categories | S3, boundaries in [[../02-Streams]] |
| **4.1 Data management** | No processes in any of the six areas; the closest thing that exists is refresh monitoring | S1: register and data contracts |
| **4.2 Content management** | Validation and release happen informally; there is no certification, no archiving, no usage measurement | Partly S3; archiving deferred until adoption is measured |
| **4.3 Insight management** | Does not exist. The commercial director's request for an assistant is an attempt to jump straight here | Assistant opening gate in [[../04-Goals]] |
| **5. Technology foundation** | The warehouse exists, the metric layer and catalog do not. The BI migration runs outside the BI team's control | S1, S2, S4 |
| **6. Operational foundation** | No governance cadence, no permission matrix, no standards. Analyst competencies are not described | S5 for permissions; the rest deliberately deferred |
| **7. Change management** | The budget is unallocated and the decision falls in November — that is the strategy's central fork | [[../index]] sponsor ask, [[../05-Risks]] |

## Blocks that reached no stream

- **Insight management (4.3)** — a deliberate decision: insights are unreachable until the first link of the chain is closed. Recorded under "what we deliberately do not do".
- **Part of the operational foundation (6)** — standards, competencies, regular management. Deferred by capacity rather than importance: a team of three cannot run them alongside S1. Revisited at the six-month re-assembly.
