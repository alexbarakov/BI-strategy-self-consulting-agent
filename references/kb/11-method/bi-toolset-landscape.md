An assessment of the applicability and potential of technical solutions. The cut: **value/applicability (high/medium/low) · consumers · technical solution · owner · cost**.

## The list of directions

- **A GPT-based solution to enforce BI UX**
- **An insight generation and management solution**
- A second BI system for a specific task or requester
- BI on mobile · BI in the cloud
- **BI inside corporate messengers** (Slack, Teams)
- **Embedding BI into corporate systems**
- Self-service ETL tools · self-service ML tools, AutoML
- **Data governance systems: metadata management and the data catalog**
- **The semantic layer**
- Alerting and mailings
- Mapping tools: from shared spreadsheets to a web mapping interface
- BI-to-Confluence integration · BI-to-glossary integration
- Tools for advanced monitoring of BI servers

## How to read the sheet in 2026

The list was drawn up before the agentic wave, but its first two entries - GPT-based UX and insight generation - are exactly where the market moved. On the same sheet, the semantic layer and the data catalog stopped being "one option among many": in the chain `core -> semantic -> context -> AI accuracy -> self-service` they are a precondition, not an option.

A practical move: fill in the sheet as it stands, then separately check the ordering - whether the "GPT-based solution" entry sits above its own link in the chain. That is precisely the `no-assistant-without-foundation` kill-gate.
